FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Will the autonomous DAST scanner take down my production website?

No. AegisAppSec employs intelligent concurrency throttles, rate limit headers, and non-destructive payload verification. Our heuristics simulate attacks without dropping database tables or generating denial-of-service traffic.

How does Target Domain Verification protect from unauthorized scanning?

Before scanning is permitted, you must verify ownership of the target domain via one of three methods: adding a cryptographic DNS TXT record, hosting a verification token under /.well-known/aegis-verification.txt, or placing an HTML <meta> tag in your homepage. This guarantees that only authorized owners can probe targets.

Can I switch between Individual and Company plans as my team expands?

Yes! You can upgrade from Pentester Pro to Team Growth with one click in your billing console. All target authorizations, historical scan data, and custom virtual patches are seamlessly migrated.

How does the WAF Virtual Patch Studio work with the scanner?

When our DAST scanner identifies an exploitable flaw (like SQLi or SSRF), our engine can automatically generate a corresponding regex virtual patch. In Enterprise mode, you can deploy this virtual patch into the live ASGI shield in milliseconds to neutralize the exploit before your engineers write a single line of backend code.
